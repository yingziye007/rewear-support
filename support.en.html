Which systems are supported?

The app minimum is iOS 17. The bundled category model requires iOS 18 or later; iOS 17 uses the manual-review path. Minimum-OS and physical-device acceptance remain incomplete. iOS 18 or later is a model requirement, not a guarantee of successful recognition on every device.

How do I add clothes?

Continue as a guest. Open Closet → Add clothes → Choose Photos, select a few images, review them, then Add / Add All. Selecting a photo is not the same as saving an item. Keep source photos and a private backup. If saving fails, retain the review entries and note the error; do not discard them as a repair step.

How do I correct a suggestion?

Open the piece and choose its category and other details. Manual corrections take precedence. Category suggestions and label reading are local and can be wrong; optional attributes are not all inferred automatically. Crop/cutout changes presentation, retaining the original. Unsupported or unreliable cutout can use the full photo; this is not generative flat-lay photography.

Why are there too few looks?

Local rules need eligible, compatible pieces: a dress or top + bottom, plus shoes. Review unknown categories and check whether pieces are archived or excluded. Two distinct looks appear only when combinations exist. Inspiration uses details you select, not automatic image understanding. Like, planning Today/Tomorrow and recording Wore are separate actions.

Where are cloud AI and weather?

Live cloud-AI styling, account sync and online weather are not connected in this beta. No weather location request or coordinate transmission is made. Looks use local category rules; check actual conditions yourself. Do not enable location as a fix for missing weather.

How do I export a backup?

You → Export or restore a backup → Prepare export → Save backup to Files. Finish imports first. Preparation alone does not save a file. Choose a private destination: the file is unencrypted, and Files providers may sync it to their cloud. The .rewearbackup format is JSON/base64, not ZIP. Limits are 256 MiB document, 192 MiB total content and 32 MiB per file; encoding overhead reduces capacity.

How do I restore or switch closets?

On the same screen, Choose backup, review the validated summary, then Restore as separate closet. Cancel does not restore. The original remains; there is no merge, and later edits are independent. Use Local closets to switch back. Keep the original backup until recovery is checked. Language changes do not convert stored currencies.

Does removal permanently delete photos?

No. Remove from Closet archives items in Recently Deleted indefinitely; Restore makes them active again. Discarding drafts or changing image presentation does not purge existing media. There is no working complete permanent-erasure feature. App deletion does not erase independent exports, source Photos or system/provider backups. Read the privacy policy for details.

Does the local beta require payment or an account?

The guest wardrobe and portable backup flow require neither. No unlimited or lifetime cloud service is promised. Apple’s TestFlight distribution and feedback processes are separate; this documentation does not grant beta access.
