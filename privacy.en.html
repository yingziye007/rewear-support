Privacy policy

Scope and contact

Rewear offline beta 0.1.0 (1). Operator: Yingzi Ye. Privacy and support: yeyingzi0706@gmail.com. Effective date: 27 October 2026.

Your local wardrobe

No online account is required. The app stores a local closet identity, clothing details and edits, optional names, notes, prices/currencies, selected original and processed images, inspiration images and chosen tags, saved outfits, plans and wear history. The selected closet is remembered in device preferences. Original imported bytes are retained and may include EXIF/location metadata; do not assume every image is stripped of metadata.

Photos and local processing

You choose images using the system photo picker, or use the available camera path with system permission. Image preparation, label-text reading and category suggestions run on the device. The bundled category model requires iOS 18 or later; iOS 17 has a manual-review path, not model inference. Suggestions may be wrong; manual correction is available. Cutout can fall back to the full photo. Inspiration matching uses tags you select, not automatic understanding of a reference image.

No connected cloud AI or weather

This beta has no connected live cloud-AI styling service, automatic closet cloud sync, advertising or app analytics/tracking service in the reviewed implementation. Online weather is disabled: normal startup, re-entry and refresh do not request weather location permission, location, place lookup or weather transport, or send weather coordinates. Looks use local category rules, not live weather. Check conditions yourself. Historical snapshots may remain. This does not mean the whole device sends no data.

Exports, restores and system copies

Backup export is your explicit action: prepare an export, then save it to a chosen Files destination. The .rewearbackup file is not encrypted by Rewear. It contains closet records and referenced media, including historical references, not necessarily abandoned/unreferenced draft files. iCloud Drive or another Files provider may upload or synchronize the file under its own policies and settings. System/device backups may also copy app data. Restore creates a separate retained closet after confirmation; it does not merge with or erase the current closet. Later edits to the two copies are independent.

Retention and deletion limits

Local records/media have no automatic expiry. Remove from Closet archives an item; Recently Deleted retains it indefinitely and allows Restore. Original photos and saved history remain. Discarding drafts removes review entries, not already-written media. Cropping, re-cutting or choosing the original does not purge older files; migration may leave a prior-schema JSON copy. Permanent-deletion groundwork is preview-only; there is no working complete in-app permanent-erasure or orphan-file cleanup feature.

App deletion is not deletion of all copies

Deleting the app can lose its local data. Offloading is different and may retain data. App removal does not delete source Photos, independent exported files, copies outside the deleted storage, or device/cloud backups. Manage those with the corresponding system/provider controls. There is no secure-erasure or erase-all-backups guarantee. Support cannot remotely erase your local wardrobe.

Support messages and Apple TestFlight

If you email support, your address, message and any attachments are disclosed to the operator and processed by email providers to deliver and handle your request. Avoid private photos and complete backups unless separately agreed. If distributed through TestFlight, Apple processes installation, crash/diagnostic and feedback information under its own TestFlight and privacy terms; some information may be available to the developer. This is separate from Rewear’s local wardrobe processing, not a promise that nothing is collected.

Questions and changes

Email the contact above for privacy questions or applicable rights requests. Include only information needed to describe the request. New cloud/weather processing would require updated disclosures and appropriate permission before activation. This policy is not legal clearance or a claim of compliance certification.
